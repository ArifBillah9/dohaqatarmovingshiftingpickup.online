Questions

Frequently asked — Office Relocation

Can you move our office outside normal business hours?

Yes, we regularly schedule office relocations in the evening, overnight, or over the weekend specifically to avoid disrupting your business operations.

Do you handle IT equipment and servers?

We carefully pack and transport desktops, monitors, servers and networking equipment. For complex IT disconnection/reconnection, we recommend having your IT team on-site during the move.

How far in advance should we book an office move?

We can often accommodate short-notice moves, but booking 1-2 weeks ahead lets us plan crew size, packing materials and timing more precisely for a smoother move.
